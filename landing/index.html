The problem

Every tender starts the same way: someone senior burns a day (or three) reading the brief, building a compliance matrix by hand, guessing at page allocation, and drafting a rough skeleton before the real proposal writing can even begin — while the clock on submission day keeps running.

What CivilProposals does

Reads your brief and produces the same first-pass output a senior engineer would spend days on: compliance matrix, weighted structure, page allocation, gap analysis, first-pass section drafts, and a themed Word export — a real starting point for your team to refine, never a submission-ready final draft.
