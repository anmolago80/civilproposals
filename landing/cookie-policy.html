Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how CivilProposals uses cookies and similar technologies on civilproposals.com and app.civilproposals.com.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They're commonly used to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, or measure how a site is used.

Cookies we use

CookiePurposeType
Session / loginKeeps you signed in to app.civilproposals.com while you use itEssential

This marketing site (civilproposals.com) does not currently set analytics or advertising cookies. The application (app.civilproposals.com) uses a single essential session cookie so you can stay logged in: without it, the Service can't tell you're signed in from one page to the next.

Managing cookies

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Since the only cookie we currently use is essential for staying logged in, blocking it will prevent the Service from working properly.

Changes to this policy

If we start using additional cookies, for example for analytics, we'll update this page and, where required, ask for your consent first.
